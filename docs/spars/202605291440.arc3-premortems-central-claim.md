# Spar: Arc 3 (premortems) central claim — decision-frameworks.md §12

**Timestamp**: 2026-05-29 14:40 CDT
**Project**: sparring
**Ceremony**: `/spar 3` (3 iterations requested)
**Topic**: The Arc 3 central claim of `framework/decision-frameworks.md` §12 — whether SPARRING's three claimed additions over a well-run premortem (iteration-to-convergence, both-must-agree signal, artifact-gated challenges) are genuinely load-bearing rather than theatrical, and whether the agent-mediation threshold holds.
**Outcome**: **Unresolved at cap — substance converged at Round 2.** The Round-3 cap-miss is a process artifact (the Generator over-edited at the buzzer; the Challenger correctly blocked it), not a substantive deadlock. The converged Round-2 wording plus the single agreed citation fix is taken as final.

---

## The claim under test (pre-spar §12.6)

> A premortem is a one-shot, single-room, ungated act of adversarial imagination; SPARRING is its enforced generalization — two distinct evidence bases, challenges gated by artifact-or-dismissed, iterated to an explicit both-must-agree convergence. The distinction collapses to nearly nothing in a good human premortem with a skeptic in the room, and becomes load-bearing the instant the skeptic is replaced by an agent that produces fluent failure modes with no stake and no evidence.

## Personas and evidence bases (per spec D2)

- **Generator** — SPARRING-internal framework specialist.
  - Evidence base: `framework/decision-frameworks.md` (§10, §12), `framework/notes.md`, the prior Torres spar artifact. Internal corpus only.
- **Challenger** — decision-science / debiasing / structured-analytic-techniques practitioner.
  - Evidence base: external published literature — Klein 2007; Kahneman 2011; **Mitchell, Russo & Pennington 1989** (prospective hindsight); **Heuer 1999** (Analysis of Competing Hypotheses); Heuer & Pherson 2010 (Structured Analytic Techniques); **Tetlock 2015** and **Kahneman & Klein 2009** (adversarial collaboration); Surowiecki 2004 (independence); **Mercier & Sperber 2011** (argumentative theory of reasoning); Janis (groupthink); Lovallo & Kahneman 2003.

The evidence bases were genuinely disjoint: the Generator confirmed its corpus contains zero references to any of the Challenger's sources. This was a real spar, not persona-only specialization theater.

## Iteration log

### Round 1 — Generator `agree: false` / Challenger `agree: false`

**Generator** proposed the claim was defensible but needed three tightenings (demote iteration-to-convergence as weakest; resolve a D2 lede/honest-statement inconsistency; soften "collapses to nearly nothing").

**Challenger** raised two artifact-backed defects the draft had missed:

1. **Source-of-power reversal.** The premortem's empirically-validated active ingredient is **prospective hindsight** — adopting the stance that the outcome has already happened and is certain (Mitchell, Russo & Pennington 1989: ~30% more causes generated under certainty framing; Kahneman 2011 endorses on exactly this basis). The source of power is the *certainty framing*, **not adversariality**. §12 generalized the adversarial axis and silently dropped the cognitive mechanism — so "SPARRING is the premortem's enforced generalization" is **inaccurate genealogy**. SPARRING is more honestly a generalization of Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (Heuer 1999) and adversarial collaboration (Tetlock 2015; Kahneman & Klein 2009).
2. **Threshold mis-attribution.** "No stake" is the wrong primary variable; **independence + grounding** is (Surowiecki 2004 — decorrelated error requires independent sources; Mercier & Sperber 2011 — a lone reasoner finds flaws in others' arguments, not its own, *regardless of stake*).

Plus: artifact-gating is **not novel** (ACH is evidence-gated prior art, fielded since the 1980s — concede it); iteration-to-convergence was **undervalued** by the Generator (iterating to a jointly-located residual-disagreement point is the *defining* feature of adversarial collaboration, not "do the premortem again"); and the "ungated, no downstream discipline" framing is a partial strawman (Klein's fielded premortem has a review/sort/address triage phase).

### Round 2 — Generator `agree: true` / Challenger `agree: false`

**Generator** conceded both hits (its corpus had no rebuttal) and repaired the genealogy **by reframing rather than abandoning**: SPARRING **keeps the premortem's social licensing** (sanctioned dissent) and **replaces its cognitive mechanism** (single-room prospective hindsight) with gate-enforced cross-examination, naming ACH + adversarial collaboration as the gates' actual lineage. This is **Arc 1's identical move** — "SPARRING invents none of the disciplines; it gate-enforces them" — which strengthened, rather than weakened, the Torres central claim. Reframed the threshold around independence+grounding, demoting "no stake" to an agent-specific amplifier. Conceded it had iteration-to-convergence backwards and moved it into the load-bearing residue.

**Challenger** endorsed all of that substance, blocking on **exactly one** issue: the revised wording cited Mercier & Sperber 2011 as the sole authority for a sentence that also asserted agents "produce fluent, ungrounded failure modes" — an LLM-confabulation claim M&S does not support. Required fix: scope M&S to the self-correction/independence half; carry the agent-confabulation premise on a sycophancy source instead. The Challenger explicitly committed to agreeing on that single fix.

### Round 3 (iteration cap) — Generator `agree: true` / Challenger `agree: false`

**Generator**, asked to apply *only* the M&S scoping fix, instead **rewrote the whole claim** — deleting the prospective-hindsight lede and the "gate-enforce, invents none of these" line, and introducing a new unvetted frame ("premortems are the *temporal variant of SPARRING's core move* … the adversary on the time axis").

**Challenger** correctly caught the over-edit: (a) it **reintroduced the exact source-of-power reversal** the spar was convened to fix (re-subordinating the premortem's validated mechanism to SPARRING's frame); and (b) it committed a **fresh Surowiecki misuse** of the same class as the original M&S error — Surowiecki's decorrelated error is aggregation across *many* independent judges; a single future-failure persona is not a crowd. Signaled `agree: false`.

## Outcome and disposition

**Unresolved at iteration cap** — but the only thing unresolved is the Generator's Round-3 over-reach, which the Challenger rightly rejected. The substance converged at Round 2, and the disposition both parties agreed to is unambiguous: **take the Round-2 wording and apply only the M&S scoping fix** (M&S scoped to the lone-reasoner/self-correction half; the agent-confabulation premise carried by Sharma 2023 / Perez 2022; Surowiecki dropped, since the N=1-persona deployment misuses it).

This is the ceremony working as designed: the both-must-agree gate (D4) and the verifiable-artifact discipline (D3) caught a last-round attempt to smuggle an unvetted, mechanism-reversing reframe past convergence. Recording the cap-miss honestly — rather than papering it over as "converged" — is itself the discipline the framework prescribes.

## Converged final wording (§12.6)

> A premortem's validated power is prospective hindsight — imagining the failure as already certain (Mitchell, Russo & Pennington 1989; Kahneman 2011) — run once, in a single room, under social license to dissent. SPARRING does not generalize that cognitive mechanism; it keeps the premortem's social licensing and replaces the single-room exercise with gate-based enforcement of disciplines that adversarial collaboration (Tetlock 2015; Kahneman & Klein 2009) and Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (Heuer 1999) already name: a distinct evidence base (D2), challenges held to artifact-or-dismissed (D3), iterated to a jointly-located convergence-or-residual-disagreement point (D4). SPARRING invents none of these — like every arc in this series, it gate-enforces them. The distinction from a good human premortem narrows to that enforced trio, and becomes load-bearing the instant the adversary is an agent: an agent lacks both an independent evidence base and the self-correction a distinct adversary supplies (Mercier & Sperber 2011 — a lone reasoner finds flaws in others' arguments, not its own, regardless of stake), and with no stake its fluent, ungrounded failure modes are cheap to produce (Sharma et al. 2023; Perez et al. 2022); the gates are what separate adversarial pressure from adversariality theater.

## What the §12 revision absorbed

- **Genealogy reframe** (§12.1–§12.2): "enforced generalization of the premortem" → "keeps the premortem's social licensing, replaces its cognitive mechanism with gate-enforced cross-examination." The premortem's validated mechanism (prospective hindsight) is named and explicitly *not* claimed as SPARRING's.
- **Prior-art honesty** (§12.2–§12.3): ACH (Heuer 1999) and adversarial collaboration (Tetlock; Kahneman & Klein 2009) named as the lineage of the gates; new table row so the gates are never implied novel. Klein-triage strawman softened (the premortem's downstream phase is single-room and plausibility-sorted, not absent).
- **Threshold reframe** (§12.4): leads with independence + grounding (Surowiecki 2004; Mercier & Sperber 2011); "no stake" demoted to an agent-specific amplifier; the "worse than nothing" line tagged internally consistent / externally unvalidated.
- **Iteration-to-convergence** returned to the load-bearing residue alongside the artifact-gate.
