## What it measures, and what it doesn't

This is a **preservation** measure: how much of a prior text a revision kept, and how much of the revision is text that wasn't there before. **It is not a measure of rewrite size.** Its one-number form (*share of prior words not kept*) can't see additions: a revision that keeps every word and appends five times as much scores 0. So every comparison here carries three numbers, and none should be quoted alone:

| quantity | formula | reads as |
|---|---|---|
| `prior_not_kept` | 1 − kept / words(prior) | how much of the prior text is gone or changed |
| `output_new` | 1 − kept / words(output) | how much of the revision was not there before |
| `length_ratio` | words(output) / words(prior), also in characters | how much it grew |

**kept** is the total size of the matching blocks from `difflib.SequenceMatcher(None, prior_tokens, output_tokens, autojunk=False)`. **Tokens** come from `str.split()`: runs of whitespace, with case and punctuation kept and no normalisation.

**The two chains.**
- **A. Final against R0.** Each condition's `final_output` against the §6.3 original (R0) of the same problem, from `catch-rate-v3-2026-06-28/recommendations.json` (the same source as `run_study.py` l.39).
- **B. The R3 patch chain.** Every R3 patch attempt against the champion in force when it was proposed. The champion starts at R0 and advances to each round's admitted candidate, as `r4.py` l.216–234 and `stage0_denoise.champion_texts` do. Admitted and rejected attempts are both listed.

**Not covered.** R1′'s first (intermediate) self-revision is not saved, so R1′ is final-against-R0 only. R2's per-round texts and R3v2 are not analysed.

## Relabelling note: `r4-results.json` → `churn_mean_chars.R3_whole_rewrite`

`r4-results.json` reports `R3_whole_rewrite: 4395.5`. **The label is wrong about what the number is.** The file itself is left unchanged; this note is the correction.
- **What the number is.** It is the mean *character churn* (characters removed plus characters added) per admitted R3 patch, against the champion it replaced, over the **same-vendor arm only** (r4.py `ARMS = ("same",)`, 25 patches). It uses a character-level `SequenceMatcher` with the default `autojunk=True`. Recomputed here: 4395.5.
- **Autojunk inflates it.** On long strings, autojunk treats common characters as junk and breaks up the matches. With `autojunk=False` the same patches give 3180.7.
- **It is mostly additions.** Same arm, `autojunk=False`: a mean of 299.4 characters removed against 2881.2 added per patch. Both arms (53 patches): 471.4 removed against 2966.0 added.
- **So it does not describe whole rewrites.** Chain B shows R3 kept most of the prior text at every patch after round 1, and grew by appending. Read the figure as *mean characters changed per admitted R3 patch, mostly added text*. The label later led a review to the false premise that *"R3's revisions were whole rewrites"*.
