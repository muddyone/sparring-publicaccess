If it doesn't hold for this study, just add "a large drop". **Impact 8**

3. "users rate sycophantic answers higher" -> "users rate flattering, agree-with-you answers higher". "Sycophantic" isn't a lay word. **Impact 7**

4. "In a "noise audit" at one insurance company," -> "In a study at one insurance company that measured how much its experts disagreed,". The quote marks can't be heard, and the term means nothing to a lay reader. **Impact 7**

7. "In two pre-registered experiments" -> "In two experiments whose methods were fixed in advance". **Impact 6**
