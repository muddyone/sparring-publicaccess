**1. The 2026 "most likely not conscious" quote is a 2023 view. Impact 8. [primary]**

**2. "Within a decade: maybe one in four" understates his figure and its date. Impact 7. [primary]**
- **What the source says (2023, around lines 660–690):**

- **Body sentence to add:** "…his own figures somewhat higher on both counts, and in mid-2023 he thought his timelines 'might even be somewhat conservative'."

**7. The survey claim. Impact 5. [primary]**
- **What the source says:**
  - N=300 US adults (Prolific, census quotas), rating ChatGPT 1–100 from "clearly not an experiencer" up.
  - "the majority (67%) attributed some possibility of phenomenal consciousness".
  - "a linear increase from 'never' to 'more than once per day'": that is correlation in one cross-sectional sample.
  - The paper does not survey experts. It contrasts its results with prior expert opinion.
- **Problems:**

- **Restatement:** "In one US study, 67% gave ChatGPT some possibility of experience, and heavier users gave more. Many people will use these systems daily, so we should expect some to believe someone is there."
