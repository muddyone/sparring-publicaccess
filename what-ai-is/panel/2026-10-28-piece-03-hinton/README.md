## The panel

Each reviewer read independently.

1. [Miriam, fidelity](1-miriam.md). The survey's dated question went to 265 of the 582
   researchers, and it asked about "today"; the survey can show Hinton is far from his field's
   typical answer, but it can't vouch for Chalmers; "not Hinton's field" became "Chalmers's field
   more than Hinton's"; "an argument, not a finding" applies to Chalmers too.
2. [Rosetta, voice and flow](2-rosetta.md). The next paragraph's "anyway" no longer followed;
   two citations would be read aloud by the narrator; credentials belong with his name; his prism
   example keeps him from sounding foolish once his scare quotes vanish aloud.
3. [Nadia, cold read](3-nadia-cold-read.md). "Outranked, not answered": the passage said he was
   outnumbered without showing where his argument is in dispute.

All three were taken. The revised passage says where the dispute lies (whether there is anything
it is like to be the chatbot when it reports how things seem), and was checked again.
