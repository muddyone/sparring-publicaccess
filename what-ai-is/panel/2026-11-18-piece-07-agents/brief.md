## 3. The register target

State the finding, then say what it means in plainer words. Checks: about 30% of sentences under eight words;
at least three explainer connectives; read every paragraph aloud. **Flow outranks the short-sentence ratio:**
connected prose, each paragraph opening from where the last ended, fragments only as a deliberate landing.
Flag a register failure the way you would flag an overclaim, in both directions: nothing demeaning toward
anyone's work (including the companies and the incident subjects named), nothing self-deprecating about ours.
Authorial presence is something to protect: it is Bart's essay, in his voice.
